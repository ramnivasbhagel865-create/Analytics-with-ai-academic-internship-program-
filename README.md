## 1. Project Overview & Problem Statement

## 3. Key Findings & Insights
- **Leading Verticals:** Technology and Finance lead overall adoption rates (>78%), primarily leveraging coding assistants and automated quantitative processing[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span).
- **Enterprise Scale Dynamics:** Large organizations maintain higher aggregate daily active user counts, but Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) showed the highest adoption growth rate across 2023–2025[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span).
- **User Sentiment:** Approximately 65% of recorded feedback is positive, with negative sentiment concentrated around onboarding and legacy tool integration hurdles[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span).
